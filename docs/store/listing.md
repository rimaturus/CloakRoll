## en-US

**Name:** Cloakroll: Encrypted Photos

**Short description:** Encrypted photo backup on your own cloud: it only ever sees noise.

**Full description:**

Cloakroll backs up your photos and videos to the cloud you already have without showing them to anyone. Each file is encrypted on your phone and uploaded as an image of random pixels, like the static of an old TV. The cloud only stores noise: only Cloakroll, with your password, turns it back into your photo.

WHY
Cloud photo services scan what you upload: faces, places, objects, text. With Cloakroll you keep the storage and take away the content: whoever gets into your account finds only images of noise.

WHERE
• Amazon Photos (unlimited photos with Prime; choose your Amazon site)
• Microsoft OneDrive and Google Drive, through their official interfaces, with access only to the app's own folder
• Or only on this phone, in a folder you choose, with no cloud at all
• Optionally an encrypted copy on the phone next to the cloud

HOW IT WORKS
• Automatic backup: new photos and videos go to the vault by themselves, on Wi-Fi or mobile data as you prefer, even with the app closed. Or pick them yourself with Android's photo picker.
• Encryption (AES-256-GCM) happens on your phone, before upload. The key comes from your password and never leaves the phone.
• Originals are downloaded only when you open them; browsing costs no data.
• Free up space: remove from the phone what the vault already holds.

FEATURES
• Folders, encrypted previews, photo and video viewer with pinch to zoom
• Fingerprint unlock, automatic lock, no screenshots
• Password change that re-encrypts the whole vault
• Restore on a new phone in seconds: sign in to the same storage and enter the same password
• Time left for every upload and download
• Light and dark theme, 6 languages

PRIVACY
No ads, no tracking, no Cloakroll accounts, no servers of ours. The app talks only to the storage you chose and sends it only encrypted files. The photo permission is asked for only if you turn on automatic backup.

OPEN SOURCE
The code is public (GPL-3.0) and the Play build includes code transparency, so anyone can check what the app does. Your files can also be decrypted on a PC with the project's free script.

IMPORTANT
• If you forget your password, your photos cannot be recovered: nobody can do it, not even us.
• Amazon has no public photos API: Cloakroll uses the same requests as the Amazon Photos website. If Amazon changes them, uploads may stop until the app is updated; stored files always stay decryptable.

Cloakroll is an independent project, not affiliated with Amazon, Microsoft or Google. Amazon Photos, OneDrive and Google Drive are trademarks of their respective owners.
